🌊 Underwater Vehicle Trajectory Planner

Learn how different algorithms plan paths for underwater vehicles

🚀 Quick Start Guide

1

Launch the Interface

Click the "Launch Planner" button below to open the 3D underwater environment. You'll see a beautiful underwater scene with animated water.

2

Place Your First Waypoint

Click anywhere in the dark blue area (the underwater space) to place your starting point. You'll see a green sphere appear - this is where your vehicle starts.

3

Add a Destination

Click somewhere else in the underwater area to place your destination. An orange sphere will appear, and the system will automatically plan a path!

4

Compare Algorithms

Use the algorithm buttons (Dubins, Bio-SALP, RRT*) on the right panel to see how different algorithms create different paths for the same waypoints.

🚀 Launch Trajectory Planner

🎯 Dubins Curves (Green)

Creates smooth, curved paths that respect the vehicle's minimum turning radius. Like a car that can't make sharp turns.
Look for: Smooth, flowing curves with no sharp angles

🐙 Bio-SALP (Orange)

Mimics jellyfish movement with pulsing, undulating motion. Uses discrete jet propulsion events like real sea creatures.
Look for: Wavy, pulsing paths that look like jellyfish swimming

🌳 RRT* (Purple)

Explores the environment randomly to find paths around obstacles. Great for complex environments with many barriers.
Look for: Paths that efficiently navigate around obstacles

💡 Pro Tips for Easy Use

  • Can't see the 3D scene? Try scrolling up/down or refreshing the page
  • Click not working? Make sure you're clicking in the dark blue underwater area, not on the control panels
  • Want to start over? Click the "Clear Scene" button to remove all waypoints and paths
  • Add obstacles: Click "Add Obstacle" then switch to RRT* to see how it navigates around them
  • Animate the vehicle: After creating a path, click "Animate Vehicle" to watch a 3D submarine follow your planned route
  • Adjust parameters: Move the "Turn Radius" slider to see how vehicle constraints affect the path shape

🎯 Try These Learning Scenarios

Scenario 1: Basic Path Planning

Place 2 waypoints far apart. Switch between Dubins and Bio-SALP to see the difference between smooth curves and pulsing motion.

Scenario 2: Obstacle Navigation

Add 2-3 obstacles, then place waypoints that require going around them. Compare how Dubins vs RRT* handle obstacles.

Scenario 3: Turning Constraints

Create a path, then adjust the turn radius slider from minimum to maximum. Watch how the path changes!

Scenario 4: Vehicle Animation

After creating any path, click "Animate Vehicle" to watch a 3D submarine follow your planned trajectory.
